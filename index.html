How to play

  1. Each sector plays in 30-second loops. When the clock runs out, time rewinds.
  2. Your last loop becomes an echo. It repeats every step, shot and ability you made.
  3. The Anchor moves and fires the same way every loop, and damage to it carries over. Destroy it to clear the sector.
  4. Each loop brings more enemies, and they can break your echoes. Keep them alive.
  5. Health refills every loop. If you die, a retake replays the loop. No retakes left ends the run.
