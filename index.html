HOW TO PLAY

THE LOOP

  • Each sector runs in 30-second loops. When time runs out, it rewinds.
  • Your last loop becomes an ECHO that repeats exactly what you did: every step, shot and ability.
  • The ANCHOR moves and fires the same way every loop, and its damage carries over. Kill it to clear the sector.
  • Each loop adds new enemies. Enemies can break your echoes, so protect them.
  • Health refills every loop. Dying spends a Chrono Charge and retries the loop.
  • Pro tip: plan ahead. Where you stand now is where an echo will fight next loop.

CONTROLS

  • Left thumb (anywhere on the left): move
  • Right thumb: drag to aim and fire. Just hold to auto-target the nearest enemy.
  • Tap the round button (bottom right) for your ability · II top-right pauses
  • WASD / arrows move · MOUSE aim · LEFT CLICK fire (hold)
  • SPACE / RIGHT CLICK / SHIFT ability
  • ESC / P pause · F toggle auto-fire · M mute
  • Gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim, RT fire, A / RB ability
